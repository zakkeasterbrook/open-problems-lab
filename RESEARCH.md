# Research ledger

The public ledger covers investigations proposed by anyone across any field, not only academic conjectures. The initial projects are examples, not limits on future work. It separates problem statements, findings, verification and publication. New practical and business projects should include a measurable outcome and suitable validation. See `research/registry.json` for a machine-readable version. New entries should identify the relevant contributors and cite their sources; foundation credit does not imply authorship of every project. Dates are initial research dates, not proof of priority.

## Status vocabulary

- **Proposed:** documented question, no claimed solution.
- **In progress:** active investigation recorded.
- **Candidate result:** written argument or counterexample, subject to review.
- **Computationally checked:** specified finite tests executed; does not prove an unbounded theorem.
- **Formally verified:** identified proof-assistant version, source, and successful replay of the entire stated theorem (including disclosed axioms).
- **Independently reviewed:** documented external reviewer or published evaluation, with permission to identify the reviewer.
- **Corrected / withdrawn:** record is preserved and the defect or correction is linked prominently.

Publication, formal verification, independent review and verified novelty are separate fields. Do not count a candidate as a solved open problem simply because a repository contains a proof draft.

## Propose the next investigation

[Submit a problem](https://github.com/zakkeasterbrook/open-problems-lab/issues/new?template=open_problem.md) or start with the [problem intake template](templates/PROBLEM.md). Questions may be practical, technical, scientific, mathematical, creative, organizational, or otherwise useful. The appropriate evidence may be a verified calculation, a proof, an evaluated prototype, a real-world measurement, or a transparent report of what remains unknown. Maintainers review proposals and update the ledger when the project record is ready; submission does not guarantee a solution.
