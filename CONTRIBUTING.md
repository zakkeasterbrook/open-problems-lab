# Contributing to Open Problems Lab

Everyone is welcome to participate. No degree, affiliation, programming expertise, paid AI subscription, or specific AI tool is required. We welcome questions from any field, practical solutions, experiments, software, domain expertise, proof criticism, replication, counterexamples and negative results.

## Get involved

- **Have a problem?** [Open a problem issue](https://github.com/zakkeasterbrook/open-problems-lab/issues/new?template=open_problem.md), or use [the intake template](templates/PROBLEM.md). Describe it in your own words; an initial question is enough to start discussion. Do not disclose private or commercially sensitive details.
- **Want to work on an existing question?** Browse [open issues](https://github.com/zakkeasterbrook/open-problems-lab/issues) or the [research ledger](RESEARCH.md), and share your proposed approach.
- **Have a result?** Submit a pull request with the explanation, evidence, and reproduction instructions.
- **Found a mistake?** [Open a verification issue](https://github.com/zakkeasterbrook/open-problems-lab/issues/new?template=replication.md) describing the affected claim, version, and reproducible witness.

## Standards for a research or solution submission

1. Search the ledger and existing work. State the intended outcome, baseline, constraints, sources, and success criteria. For novelty claims, provide the precise published statement and current-status evidence.
2. Give a self-contained explanation with assumptions and definitions. Identify the contribution relative to prior work, and choose a validation method appropriate to the field.
3. Include reproduction commands where applicable, versions, outputs, limitations, and known failures. Disclose AI assistance and the scope of human or machine verification.
4. Run `python tools/check_lab.py` and relevant project checks for repository changes. Do not edit original archived evidence to conceal a discrepancy.
5. Cite prior work and record actual contributor roles. Do not claim independent review or formal verification until it occurs. Follow the [research protocol](governance/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md).

## Collaboration and credit

Be respectful, constructive and open to correction. Critique the idea rather than the person. New contributors can ask questions without providing a proof or implementation. Maintainers review proposed public changes for relevance, evidence quality, safety, permissions and clarity; a proposal is not automatically accepted or certified.

Each project credits its own authors, contributors, reviewers (with consent), source researchers, and AI assistance. The lab's founder attribution does not replace contributor attribution.

Only publish material cleared under the [publication policy](governance/COMMERCIALIZATION.md). Do not share private data or secrets. Contributions are governed by the repository license to the extent applicable; third-party content must be appropriately licensed or linked rather than copied. Contributing does not establish a separate employment, funding, ownership or commercial partnership agreement.
