# Contributing

We welcome problems from any field, including practical and business questions, plus proof criticism, replication, counterexamples and negative results.

1. Search the ledger and existing work. Propose a problem using the issue template; give its desired outcome, baseline, constraints, sources and success criteria. For a novelty claim, provide the precise published statement and current-status evidence.
2. For a candidate result, submit a self-contained explanation with definitions and assumptions. Identify the relevant contribution relative to prior work, and specify how it was tested.
3. Include a reproducible verification command, versions, original outputs and limitations. Preserve failed tests. Disclose AI assistance and the scope of human or machine verification.
4. Run `python tools/check_lab.py` and any relevant research checks. Do not edit original archived evidence to conceal a discrepancy.
5. Cite prior work and your contribution; do not imply independent review until it actually occurs. Follow the research protocol.

Only publish material cleared under the [commercialization policy](governance/COMMERCIALIZATION.md). Report suspected errors respectfully and include a reproducible witness. Do not share private data or secrets. Contributions are governed by the repository license to the extent applicable; third-party content must be appropriately licensed or linked rather than copied.
