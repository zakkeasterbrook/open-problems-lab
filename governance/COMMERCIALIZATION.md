# Commercialization and publication policy

Open Problems Lab is a public, collaborative research and problem-solving initiative. Research may lead to public-interest solutions, community tools, future businesses, software, services, sponsorships, and applied research contracts. Participation does not require a commercial objective. Income is not assumed or guaranteed.

## Decide before disclosure

1. Classify a proposed project as public research, private commercial exploration, or review required. Do not place private client materials, unpublished invention details, personal information, or secrets in this public repository.
2. If an idea may be patentable, confidential, proprietary, or contract-restricted, consult an appropriate intellectual-property professional before releasing it. Public disclosure can affect patent rights, and AI assistance and prior work may affect inventorship and rights. Never presume ownership of another researcher's work.
3. The public repository's original materials use Apache-2.0 to the extent applicable rights are held. This is a permissive license with copyright and notice-preservation requirements and a patent grant under its terms. It does not require royalties or scholarly citations from all users. Already granted rights cannot simply be withdrawn by changing the license later.
4. Separate any future proprietary product, confidential implementation, customer data, or contract research into a suitably restricted workspace and review its terms. Public research may still be used to inform commercial work consistent with applicable licenses.

## Potential paths to revenue

- Paid implementations or hosted services that solve a validated problem.
- Research and engineering engagements, with scoped deliverables and realistic success criteria.
- Sponsorships and grants for suitable research projects.
- Support, integration, training or commercial tooling around public software.
- Eligible intellectual property licensed under separately reviewed terms.

Validate customer demand, measurable value, costs, risks, and rights before investing. Do not imply that a mathematical proof or a successful synthetic test establishes product-market fit. Relevant rights holders must authorize disclosure and any separate intellectual property arrangements. Spending, fundraising commitments, licenses outside the public repository's terms, and external agreements require authorization from the relevant parties; participation or maintainer review does not imply approval. Credit and rights remain governed by applicable law, licenses and explicit agreements. No special ownership of other contributors' work is asserted by the lab or its founder.
